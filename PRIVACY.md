# privacy policy

clutter by raideteam. last updated november 2026.

## short version

clutter collects nothing. your photos stay on your iPhone.

## what clutter does not do

- it has no server and no account.
- it has no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
- it does not send your photos or any other data anywhere.
- it does not share data with anyone.

## photos

clutter reads and changes your photo library only on the device, through Apple's Photos framework. it shows your photos and videos, adds photos to albums you pick, creates albums you name, and deletes photos only after you confirm in the system dialog. deleted items go to recently deleted, as with any other deletion on iOS.

if you use iCloud Photos, iOS may download a full-size copy of a photo or video from iCloud so clutter can show it. that download is handled by iOS, not by clutter.

## what is stored

clutter keeps one small file in its own storage on your iPhone. it holds:

- the ids of photos you already reviewed
- the ids of photos waiting in the deletion inbox
- whether "skip photos already in albums" is on

these ids are references to items in your library, not copies of your photos. "start over" clears the list of reviewed photos. deleting the app removes the file.

## children

clutter collects no data from anyone, including children.

## changes

if this policy changes, the new version will be posted here.
